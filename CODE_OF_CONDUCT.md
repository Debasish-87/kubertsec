# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of KubeRTSec pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone — regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

---

## Our Standards

### Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, backgrounds, and lived experiences
- Giving and gracefully receiving constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility, apologising when we make mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best not just for ourselves, but for the overall community
- Showing empathy and kindness towards other community members

### Unacceptable behaviour

- Trolling, insulting, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment of any kind
- Publishing others' private information — such as a physical or email address — without their explicit permission (doxxing)
- Sexual attention or advances of any kind, whether solicited or unsolicited
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or community events
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces — including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, code review comments, commit messages, and the project's documentation. It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces, such as at conferences or in social media posts made in a project capacity.

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers via:

- **GitHub:** Open a private [Security Advisory](https://github.com/Debasish-87/kubertsec/security/advisories/new) (use this for sensitive reports that should not be public)
- **Email:** Contact the maintainer directly (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for contact details)

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. The privacy and safety of the reporter will be respected at all times.

### Enforcement process

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards. Upon receiving a report, they will:

1. Acknowledge receipt within 48 hours
2. Review the report and gather any additional context needed
3. Determine whether a violation occurred and what the appropriate response is
4. Communicate the outcome to the reporter

### Consequences

Maintainers may take any of the following actions in response to behaviour they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct:

| Action | Consequence |
|--------|-------------|
| **Warning** | A private written warning explaining the violation and expected behaviour going forward |
| **Temporary ban** | Temporary suspension from contributing, commenting, or participating in community spaces |
| **Permanent ban** | Permanent removal from all community spaces for sustained, severe, or repeated violations |

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, wiki edits, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

---

## Conflict Resolution

Before escalating to a formal report, consider whether the issue can be resolved directly. Many conflicts arise from miscommunication or misunderstanding. If you feel safe doing so, a direct, respectful message to the other party may be the fastest path to resolution.

If direct resolution is not appropriate or has already been attempted without success, please proceed with a report as described above.
